# Data Analyst

**Department:** Business Intelligence
**Type:** Full-time
**Location:** Bangkok (on-site)

## About the role

Our Business Intelligence team turns raw operational data into decisions. As a Data
Analyst you will answer questions from the marketing, operations and finance teams,
build the dashboards they check every morning, and make sure the numbers can be
trusted.

## Responsibilities

- Write SQL queries against our data warehouse to answer business questions
- Build and maintain Power BI dashboards for weekly and monthly reporting
- Prepare ad-hoc analyses in Excel for stakeholders who work in spreadsheets
- Apply basic statistical reasoning to separate real signal from noise
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders and explain the caveats clearly

## Requirements

- 0 to 2 years of experience, new graduates are welcome to apply
- Solid SQL skills, including joins and aggregations
- Advanced Microsoft Excel, including pivot tables and lookup functions
- Experience building dashboards in Power BI
- Working understanding of statistics and data visualisation principles
- Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Economics, Business Analytics, Computer Science
  or a related field

## Nice to have

- Python for data work, particularly Pandas
- Exposure to A/B testing
- Experience with Tableau
- Any experience querying BigQuery
